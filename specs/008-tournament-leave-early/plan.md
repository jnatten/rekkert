# Implementation Plan: Leaving an Americano or Mexicano Early

**Branch**: `008-tournament-leave-early` | **Date**: 2026-11-21 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

**Input**: Feature specification from `specs/008-tournament-leave-early/spec.md`

## Summary

An organiser marks a player in an Americano or Mexicano as having left, from the courts screen.
They can bring the player back at any time. From the next round the draw leaves that player out,
and the courts and bench fit whoever is still there. A court the player leaves mid-round counts
as it stands, and the table keeps them with a "left after round N" label.

Reading the code (research R-1 to R-9) shaped the approach:

- **A tournament holds absences, and draws read the players present** (D-1). The roster never
  shrinks, so names, History and the Players page keep everyone. Every scheduler, the bench
  planners, the placement fit, `playableCourts` and `benchSize` ask who is present for the round
  being drawn. An away player is on no court and no bench, so the table credits them nothing
  without a line of new scoring code (R-2).
- **One new event, `setAway`, folded by a pure engine function** (D-2 to D-4). What it does is
  decided by the round in play when it folds:
  - between rounds: from the next round;
  - nothing scored yet: the round is redrawn without them;
  - points on the board: their court is confirmed where it stands, so points stop landing and
    the round closes when the others finish (R-4).

  Coming back mirrors it, and a mark undone before another round counts leaves no trace.
- **Americano draws now carry `placed`, and a carried draw is never re-partnered** (D-5). A
  departure can race a draw made out of contact. The draw keeps its line-up, and the phone flags
  the away player who is still on court, with one tap to settle it (Principle II).
- **A returner is owed neither a rest nor a game** (D-6). For the bench's bookkeeping only, they
  come back level with whoever is next due, as if they had just rested.
- **A full rotation counts pairs among those present. With fewer than four, no round is drawn and
  the courts screen offers Finish** (D-7).
- **Older builds are kept off by generalising spec 006's gate** (D-9). The protocol goes to 4. A
  state reports the protocol it needs (3 for a friendly with rules, 4 for a tournament anyone has
  left), and the host, the pair hold and the guest's offer all compare against it.
- **The confirmation is worked out by the engine** (D-12), so it says exactly what the fold will
  do, in numbers.

`TournamentNoAbsenceFingerprintTests` pins today's draws before any scheduler changes (D-13), and
`scripts/cross-build.sh` checks older builds still fold the same rounds. There are no new
targets, dependencies, entitlements or Info.plist keys. Evidence, decisions and alternatives are
in [research.md](research.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6.2 tools, Swift 6 language mode. Default isolation is `MainActor`
in the apps and in `RekkertSync`; `RekkertCore` is nonisolated.

**Primary Dependencies**: Apple frameworks only, all already in use (SwiftUI, Foundation).

**Storage**: `Tournament.absences`, optional, in `active.json`, History and the event log. It is
read with `decodeIfPresent` and written only when not empty. There is no migration: a
tournament without the key has nobody away.

**Testing**:

- Swift Testing via `swift test --package-path Packages/RekkertKit`, on macOS with no device.
- New core suites:
  - `TournamentNoAbsenceFingerprintTests` (committed first);
  - `TournamentAbsenceTests`, `AbsenceDrawTests`, `ReturnBenchTests`;
  - `AbsencePlacementTests`, `AwayTextTests`.
- Additions to `TournamentTests`, `TournamentLengthTests`, `SessionResultTests` and
  `WireCompatibilityTests`.
- Sync suites on `CourtHarness`: `TournamentAbsenceSyncTests` and `AbsenceAdmissionTests`.
- `scripts/verify.sh` is the gate, and `scripts/cross-build.sh` checks against the latest
  TestFlight builds.
- Simulators, tapped, and one real tournament evening, per [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

**Target Platform**: iPhone and iPad on iOS 26; Apple Watch on watchOS 10.0 and later; macOS 26
for package tests. The watch gets no new UI. The engine code it runs uses nothing newer than
watchOS 10.

**Project Type**: Mobile app (iPhone app, embedded watchOS app, widget extension), generated by
Tuist, over the local Swift package `RekkertKit`.

**Performance Goals**:

- A round is offered as promptly as today with up to 16 players. Presence filtering is linear,
  and the bench planners keep their fixed work budgets (R-6).
- The confirmation's preview is one fold, plus one redraw at most.

**Constraints**:

- Principle II:
  - the draw reads no score in Americano;
  - every device folds the same `setAway`;
  - no court with points ever changes teams;
  - no carried draw is re-partnered by a departure (D-5).
- No observable change to a tournament nobody leaves, nor to ladders, friendlies or matches
  (pinned by D-13 and the existing suites).
- Older builds can't be changed. They must be kept off a tournament anyone has left (FR-014).

**Scale/Scope**: 5 to about 16 players on 1 to 4 courts; up to nine guests sharing (spec 005),
each with a watch.

  only by the presence reads.
- `MatchStore` keeps one gate, now asked a general question, plus the gated action.
- The iPhone app gains one sheet, small additions to three views, and two renames.
- The watch app is untouched.
