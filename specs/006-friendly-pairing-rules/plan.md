# Implementation Plan: Pairing Rules for a Friendly

**Branch**: `006-friendly-pairing-rules` | **Date**: 2026-11-16 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)

**Input**: Feature specification from `specs/006-friendly-pairing-rules/spec.md`

## Summary

A friendly gets two kinds of pairing rule: **keep apart** (two or more players never partners)
and **keep together** (two players always partners when both play). They are set in setup,
changed in play by any phone that may run rounds, and saved with presets. The draw honours them
ahead of its existing preferences, and a round that cannot says so.

Reading the code (research R-1 to R-11) shaped the approach:

- **The draw is made on every device, inside the reducer** (R-4), so the rules must travel with
  the friendly and be read the same way everywhere. They become a field on `FriendlySession`,
  written only when there are any, so a friendly without rules encodes exactly as today (D-1).
- **Rules enter the draw in three places, and nowhere else** (D-2):
  - the bench's tie-break, among players equally due to sit out (D-3);
  - the line-up search, which compares *(rule breaks, today's cost)* in that order;
  - the four-player cycle, which replays among the line-ups that break fewest.

  With no rules each of these resolves exactly as today. A fingerprint of every draw for 2 to
  12 players over 30 rounds is pinned **before** the draw is touched, and must stay green
  (FR-005, SC-003).
- **Each drawn round records what it could not keep** (`unkept`), judged by the rules then in
  force. The phone shows it on the up-next offer and under the board (D-4).
- **A friendly draw now carries where it put everybody** (`placed`, as ladders and Mexicano
  already do). A rule change that races a draw on another device would otherwise re-partner a
  round people have walked onto (R-4, D-5; Principle II).
- **Mid-session changes reuse the existing correction path**, a `configure` carrying the whole
  friendly, as `setChangeEnds` does. They are not undoable, and are gated like other
  round-running controls (D-6).
- **The setup warning is a forecast.** The draw is played forward over one rotation of the
  bench. Setup fixes the friendly's id when the form opens, so the forecast is the draw (D-7,
  SC-008).
- **Older builds would silently drop the rules** (R-6). While a friendly has rules:
  - the host turns away guests below the new sharing protocol 3: 1.2 is told to update, and 1.1
    is told the match is full;
  - a phone holds the friendly back from its own watch while that watch lags behind 3, and says
    so on its board;
  - a guest offers the editor only to a host that understands it (D-9).

  An older build's correction carries no rules key, and leaves the rules as they were rather
  than wiping them (D-1, D-6).
- **Presets mint fresh player ids**, so their rules are remapped along with them (D-10).

There are no new event kinds, targets, dependencies, entitlements or Info.plist keys. The evidence, decisions and
alternatives are in [research.md](research.md).

## Technical Context

**Language/Version**: Swift 6.2 tools, Swift 6 language mode. Default isolation is
`MainActor` in the apps and in `RekkertSync`; `RekkertCore` is nonisolated.

**Primary Dependencies**: Apple frameworks only, all already in use (SwiftUI, Foundation).
Nothing new.

**Storage**:

- The new data is optional fields on values that are already persisted:
  - `FriendlySession.pairingRules` and `FriendlyRound.unkept`, in `active.json`, History and the
    event log;
  - `PresetConfiguration.friendly`'s `pairing`, in the presets file.
- All are read with `decodeIfPresent` and written only when not empty.

**Testing**:

- Swift Testing via `swift test --package-path Packages/RekkertKit`, on macOS with no device.
- New core suites: `FriendlyNoRulesFingerprintTests` (written first), `PairingRulesTests`,
  `FriendlyPairingDrawTests`, `PairingForecastTests`, `FriendlyPlacementTests` and
  `PairingTextTests`, plus additions to `PresetTests` and `WireCompatibilityTests`.
- New sync suites on `CourtHarness`: `FriendlyPairingSyncTests` and
  `PairingRulesAdmissionTests`.
- `scripts/verify.sh` is the gate.
- Simulators and one real-device evening, per [quickstart.md](quickstart.md).

**Target Platform**: iPhone and iPad on iOS 26; Apple Watch on watchOS 10.0 and later; macOS
26 for package tests. The watch gets no new UI. The engine code it runs uses nothing newer than
watchOS 10.

**Project Type**: Mobile app (iPhone app, embedded watchOS app, widget extension), generated by
Tuist, over the local Swift package `RekkertKit`.

**Performance Goals**:

- The next round is offered as promptly as today with up to 16 players and any rules (SC-007).
  The bench search is bounded at C(16, 12) = 1 820 combinations of three partitions each, and is
  pinned at under 0.5 s for 30 rounds on CI.
- The forecast is one rotation: at most 15 draws for up to 16 players (15 players: 15 rounds; 16:
  four). It runs on each edit.

**Constraints**:

- Principle II:
  - the draw reads no score;
  - every device folds the same rounds;
  - no drawn round is ever re-partnered by a late event, including a rule change (D-5).
- FR-005: no observable change to any friendly without rules, nor to Match, Winner court,
  Americano or Mexicano (FR-016). The shared `PairingSearch` gains a term that is always zero for
  them.
- Older builds cannot be changed. They must be kept off a friendly with rules (FR-014).

**Scale/Scope**:

- One court and 2 to about 16 players per friendly; a handful of rules.
- Up to nine guests sharing (spec 005), each with a watch.

**Older builds and Principle II**: no device that cannot draw with the rules ever holds a
friendly with rules (research D-9):
- **older guests** are turned away by the host;
- **a phone's own lagging watch** is held back by the phone, which says on its board that the
  watch needs updating;
- **an older build's correction** cannot wipe the rules, because it carries no rules key
  (D-1, D-6).

**Structure Decision**: the existing layout stays.

- The rules and every decision about them go in `RekkertCore/Friendly`, beside the friendly
  they belong to.
- The two shared scheduling pieces they touch, in `RekkertCore/Tournament`, keep today's
  behaviour by default.
- `MatchStore` gains the gate and the gated action.
- The iPhone app gains one new view and small additions to three.
- The watch app is untouched.
