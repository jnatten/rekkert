Rekkert keeps the score for padel and tennis on your phone and on your wrist at the same time. Tap either one and the other follows, so it does not matter which is nearer when the point ends.

FIVE WAYS TO PLAY

Match. Proper padel and tennis scoring: points, games, sets. Choose sets to win, games per set, tiebreaks, a super tiebreak for the deciding set, and what happens at 40-40: deuce, golden point, or the three star points the pro tour plays.

Points. One round counted up to a target, for when all you want is a number.

Winner court. Games pile up until the organiser blows the whistle, then you move up or down a court. The whistle banks the round and the next one starts at nil-nil.

Americano. Everyone partners everyone. Each round is drawn to avoid repeat partners and repeat opponents.

Mexicano. The table is re-ranked after every round and the top four meet on court one, 1 and 4 against 2 and 3.

TOURNAMENTS THAT RUN THEMSELVES

One or more courts, with standings and round history kept as you go. Player counts that are not a multiple of four rotate sit-outs by whoever has sat out least, and benched players are credited points you choose.

Score point by point, type the result, nudge it with a stepper, or pick it from a list of possible scores. In total points mode both halves always add up to the target, so one tap settles a court.

Players you have entered before come back as one-tap chips and as suggestions above the keyboard. Step back to an earlier round to see it as it was played, and reopen it to put a mistake right.

ON THE WRIST

The watch shows one court at a time. Swipe between them and score any of them. Everything that ends something, the round, the draw, the tournament, sits on its own page past the last court, so a stray tap during a rally cannot end anything.

SHARE THE MATCH WITH THE COURT

Read out a six character code and the other phones at the court can follow the same match and score it too. It runs over the local network, phone to phone. No account, no server, nothing to sign up for.

MADE FOR THE SIDE OF A COURT

Full screen fills the display with the two numbers, turns the brightness up and stops the screen sleeping, so the phone can be propped up and read from the far end. Turn it landscape and the digits grow half as large again.

The server is marked, and a small badge shows which half of the court the serve is struck from, drawn the way you will actually see it.

An optional spoken call after every point, the way an umpire would say it.

Swap serving team and swap sides for the moments when the app and reality disagree. The watch can flip the phone, so nobody has to walk over to it.

Save a setup as a preset, players and all. Presets reach the watch, so the regular Thursday americano starts from your wrist.

HISTORY

Every match and tournament you finish is kept, with how it ended and what it was played as. Open a past session to look it over, pick up one that never finished, or put a past tournament's players straight back on court.

WORKOUTS

Start a workout from your watch or your phone and the session is recorded to Apple Health as tennis, the nearest thing Health has to padel. It runs on its own: start it when you get on court, stop it when you leave, and play as many matches in between as you like, or none. It also keeps the watch awake, so a lowered wrist comes back to the score rather than to the clock.

Rekkert lists them, with how long you played, the energy and the heart rate, and under each one the matches you scored while it was running.

Nothing is asked for and nothing recorded unless you press the button.

PRIVACY

No account, no server, no tracking, no ads. Your matches stay on your own devices and go no further than the phones you hand the code to. Workouts are saved to Apple Health on your own devices, and your heart rate never leaves your watch.
