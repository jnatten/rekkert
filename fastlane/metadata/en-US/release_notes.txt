KEEP THE COURT TOGETHER BETWEEN MATCHES

When a shared match ends, the host chooses to keep sharing for the next match or stop.

Guests choose to stay for the next match or leave. When the host starts it, everyone on the share is told and follows along.

Start sharing before a match is on, so everyone can join while you set up.

Joining when no match is on waits until the host starts one, and leaving is one tap away.

A share you forget stops on its own after 30 minutes.
