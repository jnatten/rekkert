KEEP PLAYERS APART, OR TOGETHER, IN A FRIENDLY

Say who should never be partners, or who always should, to even the matches. Set it up with the players, change it during play from the match menu, and save it with a preset.

Setup tells you how often the rules can't all be kept with the players you have, and every phone says so when a round breaks one.

LEAVING AN AMERICANO OR MEXICANO EARLY

Somebody going home? Mark them under Who's here… on the courts screen. The draw leaves them out, and the courts and the bench fit whoever is still there.

A round that hasn't started is drawn again without them, and a court they leave part-way stops where it stands. They keep their points and stay in the table, marked with when they left, and they can be brought back at any time.

FAIRER ROUNDS

In Americano, whenever somebody sits out, nobody partners the same player twice before everyone has partnered everyone.

Who sits out together changes from rotation to rotation, so the same people aren't benched side by side all evening.

STEADIER SHARING

The host no longer loses track of a guest whose phone sits quietly in a bag between points.

A guest losing one of its two connections to the host no longer slows the court down.

A full match tells whoever tries to join how many are already on.

Phones and watches following a friendly with pairing rules, or a tournament someone has left, need this version.
